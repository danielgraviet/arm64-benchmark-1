Executive summary

Phoenix is a faster encode/RL box until packing, not a Vera replacement at 176+.

~2×

idle media: Phoenix ~8.6 s vs Vera ~15.3 s

Customer meaning: Turin finishes FFmpeg work much faster per job. At 88 that becomes ~9/s vs Vera ~5/s.

88

still the clean packing point on all three cells

Agent, analytics, disk, evals, and media stay usable at 88. Phoenix RL is already stretching there (0.50 s → 0.77 s).

RL cliff

Phoenix 0.50 s idle → 2.9 s at 176 → 9.9 s at 352 with 31 fails

Recommendation: do not sell Phoenix RL density past 88. Vera holds ~0.88–0.91 s through 352 with 0 fails.

What to say in the room

Phoenix (x86 Turin) wins some chip stories vs Vera (media, RL idle). Vera still wins ARM packing vs G5, and holds RL when Phoenix does not.

What not to mix

This is not “Phoenix vs Graviton on the same hypervisor.” RLP container vs linux-vm, different ISAs, native snaps vs Hub. Configuration-specific.

Vera still leads the coding-agent packing story; Phoenix sits between Vera and G5.

2 · Data and media work

Analytics stays a Vera 88 story. Media is where Turin actually wins the chip.

Analytics Idle: Vera 3.36 s, Phoenix 3.74 s, G5 4.08 s. Phoenix is not the bandwidth winner here. Sell Vera 88 × 4 GiB.

Media Idle ~8.6 s on Phoenix vs 15.3 s Vera vs 18.0 s G5. At 88: 9.0/s vs 5.0 vs 4.4. First Phoenix 176 dropped 42 creates; retry was clean at 15.4/s — packing jitter, not FFmpeg.

Media claim hygiene

Jobs are ~9–21 s, so jobs/s look “small.” Vera 176 still has 52 fails — do not quote 5.6/s. Phoenix retry c=88 had 1 fail; c=176 was 1408/1408. Native snap vera-media-benchmark-us-phoenix-1 (Hub media is arm64-only).

3 · Disk and filesystem

Both RLP cells stay flat on disk work. Vera is still the faster FS box.

G5 is the contention story

Phoenix 352

1 fail, p50 duration still 675 ms, tput 15.8/s. Same create-tax pattern as Vera (19.5/s at 352, 0 fails).

5 · Terminal-style evals

Evals is the cleanest Phoenix packing result in this pack.

Phoenix 88 42.4/s vs Vera 39.0/s vs G5 15.8/s. Phoenix even holds 44.5/s at 176 with duration 1.47 s.

Use evals as “Phoenix can pack short terminal work.” Do not generalize that to RL or media 176 without the failure notes on those slides.

Decision guide

Sell Phoenix for encode and short evals; sell Vera for ARM packing and RL density.

AgentLead Vera at 88 (24.7/s). Phoenix is close (21.5/s), G5 is not (11.1/s).
AnalyticsStill Vera 88 × 4 GiB. Phoenix is mid-pack, not the DuckDB winner.
DiskBoth RLP cells stay flat; Vera is faster (~430 vs ~670 ms). G5 duration is the warning slide.
MediaPhoenix is the encode chip (~8.6 s idle, ~9/s at 88). Treat 176 as retry-sensitive.
RLQuote Phoenix only through 88. The cliff after that is the product finding.
EvalsPhoenix matches or beats Vera on this short terminal mix, 0 fails through 176.
