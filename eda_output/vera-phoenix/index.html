-E

Executive summary

Phoenix wins some idle chips. Vera holds packing. 0.125 does not buy a free 8× on agent.

~2×

media idle: Phoenix 8.6 s vs Vera 15.3 s

Turin also wins idle RL (0.50 s vs 0.88 s) and evals packing at 88 (42.4/s vs 39.0/s).

flat

Vera RL ~0.88–0.91 s through 352, 0 fails

Phoenix RL is 0.77 s at 88, then 2.9 s at 176, 9.9 s + 31 fails at 352. That is the density cliff.

0.125

agent chip ≈ 1 vCPU on both cells through 88

Vera 0.125 is clean through 176. Phoenix 0.125 is clean through 176 on the retry, then toolbox :2280 timeouts at 528+.

Say in the room

Use Phoenix for encode and short evals. Use Vera when you need ARM packing that stays the same chip at 176–352.

Do not say

That 0.125 CPU × 8 is free density. Agent duration barely moved vs 1 vCPU; Phoenix 528+ failed after create, not at quota.

3 · Where 0.125 actually broke

Phoenix 528+ is a dataplane miss after create. Vera’s first miss was leftover VMs.

Phoenix first 0.125 ladder

514 fails

192 at 528 + 322 at 704 · file …212831…

Every failed job still has a sandbox_id. Error: bad gateway: dial tcp 10.102.x.x:2280: i/o timeout. Create worked; toolbox could not reach guest :2280. Distinct overlay IPs. Jobs that ran stayed ~3.3 s chip.

Vera ops

375

leftover VMs listed after overlapping ladders

~80 error + ~295 started from a wave that never tore down. Second run got no matching capacity. After delete, list was all destroyed and the 176 ladder ran 0-fail.

Two different bugs. Phoenix 528+ is not “out of CPU quota.” Vera 0.125 352+ was never rerun after the cleanup — only through 176.
