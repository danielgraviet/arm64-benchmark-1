The words you need

Reinforcement learning is a loop. Memorize these six terms and the rest of the lab will click.

Environment
The world. Ours is a mocked latent “grid” — a vector that evolves with each action. No game, no network.
Observation
What the agent sees each step — a float vector of size obs dim (we use 384 in production).
Action
What the agent does. We pick one of 16 discrete actions with argmax over policy logits.
Reward
A scalar “how good was that?” signal from the env. We sum a discounted return over the episode.
Policy
The brain: observation → logits → action. Ours is a 3-layer NumPy net (no GPU, no training in this bench).
Episode / horizon n
One rollout = reset, then n sequential steps. Harness flag --n is this horizon.

Why we chose what we chose

Map the lab back to the GTC charts.

Chart A — is the chip faster?

Heavy episode (--n 5000, batch 8, wide net). Measure only duration_ms. Optionally -E 8 so warm wall time ≈ duration (create stripped).

Chart B — can Daytona pack work?

Light episode (--n 64), many sandboxes, -E 1. Throughput + p99 latency matter; duration should stay flat at 88.

We also stopped stacking full trajectories at huge n — that was allocator noise, not “customer RL.” Running stats are enough for a checksum.
